New today

text-align

Moves your text to the left, center, right, or makes both edges straight with justify.

<h1 style="text-align:center;">Happy UAE National Day!</h1>

Try it: press a value

Where do we use them?

left · normal text
center · titles, invitations
right · dates, prices
justify · newspapers: both edges of a long paragraph are straight

Golden rules

  1. Write it in the start tag
  2. Spell center, not centre
  3. Use a hyphen: text-align
  4. : between property and value, ; between two properties
  5. justify shows best on long paragraphs
  6. align="center" is old. Use style

UAE Connect

Arabic is written right to left, so Arabic pages are right-aligned.
